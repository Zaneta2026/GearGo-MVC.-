# Analyse the Business Problem

## Problem Statement:
GearGo needs a more efficient way of managing their equiptment-rental process. 

### Affected Users:
- Customers need to be able to browse available equiptmen and request rentals. 
- Staff need to manage equipment and process rental requests. 
- Managers also need adminstrative control over the catalogue and users.

### Business Impact:
- Equiptment availability can be difficult to track and rental requests may be delayed or missed. 
- Staff may have difficulty keeping equipment information up to date. 
- Managers may have limited control and visibility over users and equiptment catalogue.  

### Proposed solution boundary:
GearGo will develop an equipment rental management system that will allow:
- Customers to view available equipment and submit rental requests.
- Staff to manage equipment and rental requests.
- Managers to manage the rquipment catalogue and users.

## User roles and use cases:
### Customers
- Browse Equipment
- View Equipment Details
- Submit Rental Request

### Staff
- Manage Equipment
- View Rental Requests
- Approve/Rejact Rental Requests

### Managers
- Manage Equipment Catalogue
- Manage Users
- View Rental Activity/Reports
